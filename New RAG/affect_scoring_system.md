AFFECT RANKING SCORING SYSTEM  
Use this scoring system to evaluate how a person feels about any object based on the ranked order of nine core affects.

## Affects Included
Curiosity  
Joy  
Surprise–Startle  
Fear  
Anger  
Sadness  
Disgust  
Pulling-Away  
The Drop  

## Valence Directions
- +1 → Curiosity, Joy  
- 0 → Surprise–Startle, Pulling-Away  
- -1 → Fear, Anger, Sadness, Disgust, The Drop  

## Ranking Rule
Rank 1 = most strongly felt  
Rank 9 = least strongly felt  

## Step 1: Convert Rank to Weight
Weight = (Total Affects + 1) - Rank

## Step 2: Compute Each Affect’s Score
Affect Score = Weight × Valence Direction  

## Step 3: Compute the Overall Relationship Score
Overall Relationship Score = SUM(all Affect Scores)

## Step 4: Normalize to a 0–100 Index
Normalized Score = ((Overall Score - (-35)) / 52) × 100

## Interpretation Guide
80–100 → Strong positive  
60–79 → Positive leaning  
40–59 → Mixed  
20–39 → Negative leaning  
0–19 → Aversive

## Short Version Snippet
Rank affects 1–9. Weight = 10 – Rank.  
Valence: Curiosity +1, Joy +1, Surprise–Startle 0, Pulling-Away 0, Fear -1, Anger -1, Sadness -1, Disgust -1, The Drop -1.  
Score = Weight × Valence.  
Normalized = ((Score + 35) / 52) × 100.
