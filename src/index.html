The Realness Score

Every one of us moves through life balancing two forces: the part we show the world, and the part we actually feel. From birth, our bodies speak in core signals—pulls, jumps, sparks, drops—that shape how we protect ourselves, connect with people, and script our emotional habits long before we ever choose them. Over time, those scripts become invisible rules: what we reveal, what we hide, what we fake, and what we feel for real.

This assessment helps you see where you land on that spectrum of fake or feeling. In just 40 quick questions, you'll discover how your core feeling patterns shape your emotional style, your defenses, and your relationships. The result is your unique Realness Archetype—a map of how your inner world shows up (or doesn't) in the moments that matter most.
