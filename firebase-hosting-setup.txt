FIREBASE HOSTING SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
====================================

Since you're at an interactive prompt, follow these steps:

STEP 1: Create the site
------------------------
At the prompt asking for site ID, type:
  realness-score
Then press Enter

STEP 2: Public directory
-------------------------
When asked "What do you want to use as your public directory?"
Type:
  dist/standalone
Then press Enter

STEP 3: Single-page app
------------------------
When asked "Configure as a single-page app (rewrite all urls to /index.html)?"
Type:
  Yes
Then press Enter

STEP 4: Automatic builds
-------------------------
When asked "Set up automatic builds and deploys with GitHub?"
Type:
  No
Then press Enter

STEP 5: Deploy
--------------
After setup completes, run:
  firebase deploy --only hosting

Your app will be live at:
  https://realness-score.web.app
